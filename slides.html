02 • Problem Identification

The 3-App Dilemma in Modern Gym Fitness

01

Tool Fragmentation

Athletes currently juggle 3 to 4 distinct apps: a spreadsheet for weights & sets, a timer app for rest, and notes for gym fees. There is no unified single source of truth.

High Cognitive Load
02

In-Gym Execution Friction

During heavy sets, athletes have seconds to log progress. Cluttered forms, modal-heavy apps, and sudden offline lockout disrupt focus and workout momentum.

Breaks Flow State
03

The 6-Month Dropout Rate

Over 50% of new gym members quit in under six months due to lack of streak accountability and financial neglect regarding recurring gym & nutrition costs.

Habit Collapse

🎯 Core Objective: Build an ergonomic, zero-friction system that unifies Training, Attendance & Finances.

03 • Proposed Solution

IronPulse: The Unified Performance Triad

🏋️ Granular Training

  • ✔ Progressive Overload: Logs weight, reps, sets & total volume per muscle group.
  • ✔ Muscle Splits: Structured presets for Push/Pull/Legs, Arnold Splits & custom routines.
  • ✔ Interactive Set Checks: Tap set pills to complete with strike-through and rest prompt.

📅 Discipline & Streaks

  • ✔ Automated Attendance: Logging any session auto-registers your gym visit.

💳 Fitness Finances

  • ✔ Dual-Pillar Budget: Separate tracking for gym fees vs. dietary/supplement expenses.

  • ✔ Payment Audits: Instant tracking of paid, pending, and overdue dues.
04 • Ergonomic Innovations

Engineered for Single-Thumb In-Gym Handling

⌘K

Spotlight Command Hub (Ctrl+K)

A Raycast-style instant command palette accessible via shortcut or header button. Filter routines, execute timers, or jump between tabs in under 0.5 seconds with zero scrolling.

🎙️

Hands-Free Voice Protocol (Hotkey: V)

Zero-touch in-gym voice assistant using Web Speech API & SpeechSynthesis. Supports natural voice commands: "start timer 90s", "check in today", "calculate bench 80 for 5", and "open workouts".

⏱️

Picture-in-Picture Floating Timer HUD

Eliminates the pain of switching apps. When the timer runs, a sleek glass pill docks above the navigation dock across all screens with live countdown, +30s boost, and chime alert.

⚡

Floating Quick Action Speed-Dial (FAB)

Pinned at the bottom-right for instant one-thumb reach. Opens 1-tap shortcuts for logging sets, voice assistant, 1RM calculator, 90s rest, and gym check-in with Web Audio tactile feedback.
